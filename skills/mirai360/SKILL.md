# Mirai360

Mirai360 is an operating partner that builds AI systems with your team for routine work, runs them in your cloud and reports one number weekly.

## When to use

- An insurer, law firm, online retailer or manufacturer wants an AI system for routine work that grows with every new client: claims intake, new-matter intake and conflict searches, returns, or turning requests for quotation into quotes.
- A COO or business-unit head wants that system built with their team, run in their own cloud and reported against a measurable outcome every week, rather than a software licence.
- A leadership team has to decide whether its own engineers build an AI system, a partner builds it with them, or they buy a product, and wants the decision in writing with the reasons.
- Nobody has pinned the problem down yet. We start with discovery: interviews with the team and a look at the systems and data, ending in a written problem, outcome and next step that the company keeps.

## Not a fit

- Selling you a chatbot or a software licence.
- Standing in as your permanent CTO.
- Supplying engineers by the hour.
- Any system that would take a decision or a signature away from a pharmacist, an underwriter or a lawyer.

## How to start

- [Find your bottleneck](https://mirai360.ai/tools/bottleneck-finder/): Ten questions about one task the team repeats; shows whether AI fits it and the number that would prove it worked.
- [Book a 30-minute fit call](https://calendly.com/shivang-mirai360/30min): The buyer tells us the most expensive version of the problem and leaves knowing whether discovery is needed.
- [Email](mailto:contact@mirai360ai.in): We reply in writing.

## Fees

Fees are fixed in writing for each engagement: a fixed fee for the first fix and a monthly retainer after it. We do not publish prices, and we are not paid by the hour.
